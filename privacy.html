Privacy

No sign-up is needed. Your phone gets a random ID stored on the phone so it can't ping the same station over and over; that ID is never shown publicly.

A ping stores the station, its location, the time, and your optional note. Your own GPS position is used on your phone to find nearby stations and is not saved with the ping.

If you use the Pizza of the Week free entry or send a station dispute, your name and contact are stored privately and used only to reach you.

Maps and station data come from OpenStreetMap contributors.
