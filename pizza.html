Pizza of the Week

Each week, Walt Henderson Jr, who runs FuelWatch, buys one active FuelWatch reporter a pizza as a thank-you.

  • No purchase or ping needed to be picked. Anyone can use the free entry form on the Pizza tab, once a day.
  • Walt picks the winner each week and contacts them using the phone or email they left. If he can't reach the person within a few days, he may pick someone else.
  • The prize is one pizza (or a pizza gift card of similar value). There is no cash prize and no cash alternative.
  • Names and contact info are private and are only used to reach the winner.
  • Fake or spam pings don't help anyone get picked and can get a phone blocked.
  • FuelWatch may change or end Pizza of the Week at any time.
