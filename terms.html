Terms

FuelWatch is a free tool for drivers to report fuel that hit the ground at a gas station. Pings are anonymous, public, and are reports from users, not verified facts. Only ping what you actually see. Don't post names, plates, or personal info in notes.

Station owners can mark a spill cleaned up with a station code, or send a dispute from the Desk. Reports are never removed for payment.

FuelWatch is not an emergency service. For fire or danger call 911. For a large spill call the National Response Center at 1-800-424-8802.

The app is provided as-is, without warranties.
